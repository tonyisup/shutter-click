## Description

```text
Take a photo with your Pixel phone's camera by pressing START on your watch.

Shutter Click is an accessibility remote for people who find it hard to reach, hold or press the phone's on-screen shutter. Frame your shot in Pixel Camera, then press START or tap SHOOT on the watch. The watch vibrates and shows Sent when the phone has pressed the shutter.

REQUIRED: the Shutter Click Android companion app
PLAY_URL

You also need:
• A Google Pixel phone with the Pixel Camera app, on Android 13 or later, with the phone language set to English
• Your watch paired in Garmin Connect

HOW TO USE
1. In the phone app, finish setup, tap Start remote, then Open Pixel Camera. Stay in Photo mode.
2. Open Shutter Click on the watch and wait for Ready.
3. Press START or tap SHOOT. One press takes one photo.
4. Press BACK to exit.

The watch tells you what to do if it cannot take a photo: Start remote, Open Camera, Unlock phone, Use Photo mode, or Check camera. If it shows No reply, a photo may have been taken; the app never retries by itself.

It does not work with iPhones, other Android phones, or other camera apps. No account, no ads and no data collection. Free and open source.

Shutter Click is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Garmin or Google.
```
