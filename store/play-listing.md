## Full description (4000 max)

```text
Shutter Click lets you take a photo with your Pixel's camera by pressing a button on your Garmin watch.

It is an accessibility tool. If reaching, holding or pressing the phone's on-screen shutter is difficult, because of limited hand mobility, tremor or reach, or because your phone sits on a mount or stand, Shutter Click presses the shutter for you. Frame the shot in Pixel Camera as usual, then press START on your watch.

HOW IT WORKS
• Start a remote session in Shutter Click and open Pixel Camera in Photo mode.
• Open Shutter Click on your watch. When it shows Ready, press START or tap SHOOT.
• The phone presses Pixel Camera's own shutter button. The watch vibrates and shows Sent.

Pixel Camera still does everything else: lenses, processing, settings and saving. Your photos stay where they always are.

WHAT YOU NEED
• A Google Pixel phone with the Pixel Camera app, on Android 13 or later
• Phone language set to English (other languages are not recognized yet)
• A Garmin Forerunner 955 or 955 Solar, paired in the Garmin Connect app
• The Shutter Click watch app from the Garmin Connect IQ Store

Tested on a Pixel 11 Pro Fold with a Forerunner 955 Solar. It does not work with other camera apps or other phone brands.

BUILT TO BE SAFE
• One press, one photo. It never repeats a shot by itself.
• It works only while the phone is unlocked and Pixel Camera is on screen in Photo mode. It will not start a video recording.
• A session ends when you end it, or after ten minutes without a photo.

ACCESSIBILITY SERVICE
Shutter Click uses Android's AccessibilityService API for its core function: finding Pixel Camera's Photo shutter button and pressing it when you ask from your watch. The service looks only at Pixel Camera's on-screen controls and acts only during a session you start. It does not read other apps, your photos, or the camera image.

PRIVACY
No ads, no account and no analytics. The app has no internet permission and collects no data.

OPEN SOURCE
Shutter Click is free software under the GNU GPL. Source code: https://github.com/tonyisup/shutter-click

Shutter Click is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Garmin or Google. Garmin, Forerunner and Connect IQ are trademarks of Garmin Ltd. or its subsidiaries. Google Pixel is a trademark of Google LLC.
```
